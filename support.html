Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings within the app and tap "Reset All Progress." This will clear all your known rules, mastered rules, practice bank, and statistics. This action cannot be undone.

Can I transfer my progress to a new device?

Progress is currently stored locally on your device and cannot be transferred between devices. We hope to add sync in a future update.

The text-to-speech voice sounds wrong or isn't working

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. To troubleshoot:

  • iOS/macOS: Go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content and make sure a voice is downloaded for your selected language.
  • Android: Go to Settings → Language & Input → Text-to-Speech and ensure a TTS engine is installed and updated.

What's the difference between US, British, and Australian English?

Some grammar rules differ by region. For example, the strict that vs. which distinction in restrictive clauses is mainly an American convention; British and Australian usage accepts which freely. The app lets you pick your region in Settings and tailors the curriculum to match. Switching regions keeps your progress on rules that apply to both.

How does the practice bank work?

When you answer a rule incorrectly, it's added to your practice bank. The app uses spaced repetition to bring those rules back at optimal intervals. A rule is considered mastered after 3 consecutive correct answers on different example sentences.

Why are there three exercise modes?

Multiple choice trains recognition, spot-the-error trains your editing eye, and fix-the-sentence trains active production. Each mode unlocks for a rule once you've mastered the previous one. Lower levels stick to multiple choice; higher levels progress through all three.

How does "Find Specific Grammar Rule" work?

The browser lets you search the full catalogue and jump straight into focused practice on one rule. It cycles through every exercise for that rule, sending incorrect ones to the back of the queue until you've answered every exercise correctly at least once. Available to premium users.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app and go to the upgrade screen. Tap "Restore Purchase" to restore your premium access using the same Apple ID or Google account you originally purchased with.

I want a refund
